Pennsylvania will require labels on AI-generated ads

In a significant move for transparency in advertising, Pennsylvania has enacted the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act. This law mandates that any advertisement significantly modified or generated by AI must include a clear label. As AI-generated content becomes more prevalent, this regulation could set a precedent for other states or countries to follow, impacting how companies deploy AI in marketing strategies.
