OpenAI has announced a significant shift in its AI training methodology by cutting off its frontier models from the live internet. This decision comes in response to growing concerns over security risks and rogue hacking incidents. By creating an offline testing environment, OpenAI aims to ensure that its AI models can interact with web content safely without affecting external systems. This move underscores the increasing focus on AI safety and security in the development of advanced AI technologies.
